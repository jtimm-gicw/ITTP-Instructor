## 🔑 Vocabulary 

### 🔁 **Loop**
Repeats a set of instructions until a stopping point is reached.  
**Analogy:** Like doing laps until you're done.

---

### 🔄 **While**
A loop that repeats **while** a condition is true.  
**Analogy:** “While it's raining, keep using your umbrella.”

---

### 🔢 **For**
A loop that repeats a **specific number of times**.  
**Analogy:** “Do 10 jumping jacks.”

---

### ❓ **Condition**
A true/false test that determines whether a loop or if-statement runs.  
**Analogy:** “If the door is unlocked, go inside.”

---

### ➕ **Increment**
To increase a number (usually by 1).  
Example: `i++`

---

### ➖ **Decrement**
To decrease a number (usually by 1).  
Example: `i--`

---

# 📘 Questions & Answers

---

## **1️⃣ What is an expression in JavaScript?**
An expression is **any piece of code that produces a value**.  
Example: `5 + 3`, `"Hi" + "There"`, `age >= 18`

---

## **2️⃣ Why would we use a loop in our code?**
To **repeat tasks automatically** without writing the same line of code many times.

---

## **3️⃣ When does a for loop stop executing?**
A for loop stops when its **condition becomes false**.

---

## **4️⃣ How many times will a while loop execute?**
As many times as its condition remains **true**—could be once, many times, or forever if not written correctly!
